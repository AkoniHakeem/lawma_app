User Manual

WastePro: a friendly tour of your platform.

Whether you run a waste management business or you are a property owner paying for the service, this guide walks you through every screen, button, and workflow you will use day to day.

Welcome

WastePro is a web-based platform that helps waste operators manage properties, generate bills, collect payments, and stay in close contact with their subscribers. Subscribers (called service clients) get their own portal to view bills, make payments, and track their account.

You don't need to install anything. WastePro runs in any modern browser and works just as well on phones and tablets as on a laptop.

Heads upYou can install WastePro to your home screen on mobile or as a desktop app. Look out for the install prompt the first time you visit.

Who uses WastePro

WastePro has three lanes. The screens, menus, and permissions you see depend on which lane you log in through.

business

Waste Manager / Operator Staff

Runs the operation. Manages properties, generates bills, collects payments, exports reports, and (for admins) controls who else can access the system.

home

Service Client

The property owner or resident receiving the waste service. Views bills, sees payment history, gets notifications, and can pay through a virtual account.

how_to_reg

New Operator (Onboarding)

A waste management company applying to join WastePro for the first time. Goes through a public registration and data-upload flow before receiving credentials.

Signing in

Open your browser and visit your operator's WastePro URL (for example, wasteproutils.com). Tap or click loginAccess App from the landing page, and you will land on the sign-in screen.

The sign-in screen has two tabs at the top:

  • Waste Manager — for operator staff. Sign in with your email and password.
  • Service Client — for property owners and residents. Sign in with your property code and phone number.

Step by step

  1. Pick the right tab for your role (Waste Manager or Service Client).
  2. Fill in the two fields. Use the visibilityeye icon next to the password field to see what you typed.
  3. Tap loginSign In to Dashboard. WastePro will check your credentials and take you to the right home page.
Forgot your password?Service Clients can sign in any time using their property code and phone number, no password needed. Waste Manager users should contact their operator's admin to reset access.

Dashboard · Super-admins only

Your at-a-glance home. Because it surfaces system-wide financial metrics, the Dashboard is restricted to super-admins. Other staff land on Properties & Billings as their default home page.

The top of the page greets you with a welcome card. Below that, four large metric cards summarise the health of your operation:

  • Total Subscribers — everyone registered to receive service.
  • Streets Managed — the streets covered by your operation.
  • Properties — properties on file across all streets.

What to do hereUse the dashboard as your morning check-in. If a number looks off, click into the relevant page (Properties & Billings, Payments, etc.) to dig deeper.

Properties & Billings

This is the busiest page in WastePro. Almost every operator workflow either starts or ends here: registering properties, looking up subscribers, generating bills, finding defaulters, and adjusting balances. Take your time with this section — everything here is connected.

The page header

The top of the page shows the title Property Billing Management and a single context-aware action button on the right that changes label and icon depending on which tab is active:

  • On the Properties tab the button reads add_businessAdd Property and opens the New Property Subscription dialog.
  • On the Billings tab the button reads printPrint Bill and opens the Generate / Print Bill panel.

The two tabs

Just below the header sit the two tabs. Each has a smart badge:

  • home_workProperties — with an orange badge showing total properties (e.g. 2,712).
  • receipt_longBillings — with a red badge showing total arrears in naira (e.g. ₦64M).

Inside the Properties tab

This is your master list of every property in your operation. The card titled Properties Overview contains a search input on the right and a paginated table below.

The search box

Type any text into searchSearch properties... and the table filters live (with a 300ms debounce so it doesn't flicker as you type). It searches across property name, old code, street, and ID. The little close× at the end of the box clears the filter.

The Properties table columns

Each row tells a complete story about a property:

  • Property ID — a blue-grey chip with a tagtag icon.
  • Property Name — bold text identifying the property (e.g. Olu's Plaza).
  • Old Code — a purple badge holding any legacy code carried over from spreadsheets or LAWMA records.
  • Street Number — the door / plot number with a homehome icon.
  • Street Name — the street with a location_onpin icon.
  • Arrears — an outstanding-balance chip that colours itself based on the amount: green when settled, amber when small, red when significant.
  • Actions — three round buttons covered next.

The three row-action buttons

At the end of every row you'll find a trio of icon buttons. Each one opens a dedicated full-screen dialog:

visibility

View Details (blue eye)

Opens the Property Details dialog — the complete profile of the property, its custodian, its finances, and its billing line items.

payment

View Payments (green card)

Opens the Payment History dialog — every payment ever recorded against this property, with filters and CSV export.

receipt

View Monthly Billings (blue receipt)

Opens the Billing History dialog — every bill issued to this property, with summary cards and an enable/disable billing toggle.

What you can do from inside the Property Details dialog

This dialog is more than a read-only profile — it's a working surface. It is divided into four sections:

  1. Property Information — ID, Name (click the editedit icon next to the name to rename in place), Street Address, Ward, LGA, and State. Action icons in the section header let you jump to paymentsView Payments, receiptView Billings, or deleteDelete Property Subscription.
  2. Financial Information — Outstanding Balance (with an editedit button that opens the Edit Arrears dialog) and Last Payment.
  3. Custodian Information — the contact responsible for the property: name, email, phone (also editable in place), and the dedicated virtual account if one has been issued.
  4. Billing Details — a table of property-type line items (e.g. 3 residential units at ₦5,000 each). To change them, click editedit in the section header. You can then:
    • Click addadd to insert a new row.
    • Click the inline editedit on a row's property type to swap it for another from the catalog.
    • Hover a row to reveal a deletedelete menu for that row.
    • Click savesave when you're done. While you have unsaved edits a small orange "Unsaved Changes" badge appears next to the section title.
    • Click closecancel to discard everything.
About inline editsProperty name and custodian phone are edited via popovers — click the field, type, and press Enter. The change saves immediately. Outstanding balance opens a dedicated dialog where you must enter both an amount and a reason before saving.

What you can do from the Payment History dialog

This dialog is full-screen. The header is a purple gradient with the property name. Inside you get:

  • Filters — eventYear and calendar_todayMonth dropdowns, plus a refreshReset Filters button.
  • Summary cards — Total Payments, Total Amount, Average Payment, and Last Payment date.
  • Payments table — columns for Payment Date, Amount (green chip), Payer Name, Month, Year, Comments, and an Actions column with a deletedelete button.
  • Footer actions — downloadExport to CSV generates a CSV scoped to the property you're viewing.

What you can do from the Billing History dialog

Also full-screen. Across the top there's a Billing Status card showing whether billing for this property is Active or Disabled, with a one-click toggle to switch:

  • Click blockDisable Billing to stop new bills from being generated for this property.
  • Click check_circleEnable Billing to turn it back on.

Below the status card you get the same Year / Month filters, summary cards (Total Billings, Total Amount, Average Billing, Latest Billing), and a billings table with columns for Billing Period, Amount, Previous Arrears, Date Created, and a Status badge that flags duplicate bills.

Why duplicate-bill flags matterIf a billing run accidentally fires twice for the same period, the second bill is flagged "Duplicate" so you can spot it. Use the row menu to remove duplicates (see Billing Details further down).

Adding a new property

Clicking add_businessAdd Property in the header opens the New Property Subscription dialog. The form is split into two columns.

Left column · Property Information

  • Property Name * — how the property will be referred to.
  • Property Unit * — how many units make up the property (1 for a single home, several for a compound or apartment block).
  • Expected Monthly Cost — auto-calculated for you. WastePro multiplies the property type's unit price by the number of units. Read-only.
  • Old Code — an optional legacy code carried over from previous records.

Right column · Location & Details

  • Street * — type to search. If the street isn't on file, an inline addAdd New Street button is right there. The little + button next to the dropdown does the same thing.
  • Street Number — the door or plot number.
  • Property Type * — pick from the catalog. The dropdown shows the type name and unit price (Residential — ₦5,000). If the type is missing, click addAdd Property Type.
  • Custodian * — the subscriber responsible for the property. Same pattern: type to search, or click addAdd New Subscriber if they don't exist yet.
  • Property Owner checkbox — tick this if the custodian is also the owner.

Once everything required is filled in, click saveCreate Property. The button stays disabled until the form is valid.

About the inline "+" buttonsThe Add New Street, Add Property Type, and Add Subscriber side-buttons each open their own small dialog. When you save the new item, WastePro reopens the New Property Subscription form automatically and refreshes the relevant dropdown so the new entry is ready to pick — no page reload needed.

Inside the Billings tab

This tab is the operational hub for everything that happens after a bill is created. The card titled Billing Management has a calendar_monthSelect Month dropdown on the right that filters the entire view to a single billing period.

The three views inside Billings

The main area can show one of three tables. You always start on the Billing Overview; the other two are reached by drilling in. A arrow_backBack to Billing Overview button appears when you've drilled down.

1 · Billing Overview (per street)

Rows are grouped by street, not by property, so you can see at a glance how each street is performing:

  • Street Name — bold text with a pin icon, plus a small caption "Click to view billing details".
  • Total Billing — sum of bills for this street in the selected month, with a receipt icon.
  • Arrears — outstanding chip that turns red when significant.
  • Actions column — covered next.

2 · The street row menu

Each street row has a unified action area. Hover the row to reveal coloured shortcut buttons; or click the more_vertmore button at any time to open the menu. There are two actions:

  • visibilityView Details — switches the area to the Billing Details table for that street, listing every bill in the selected month.
  • peopleGet Defaulters — switches to the Payment Defaulters table, listing properties on this street that haven't paid yet.

Both items show a small spinner while loading. The drilled-down table includes a back link, table title, and short subtitle so you always know where you are.

3 · Billing Details table

When you've drilled into a street's billing details, each row is one bill with these columns:

  • Property info (name, street number, custodian).
  • Total Billing with a receipt icon.
  • Arrears chip (red > 0, green = 0).
  • An Actions column with a deletedelete button to remove a bad bill (most often used to clean up duplicates).

4 · Defaulters table

Lists every property on the street that hasn't paid for the selected period. Use this list as your daily call sheet for collections. Reminder: switching back to the overview is one tap on arrow_backBack to Billing Overview.

Generating and printing bills

While on the Billings tab, clicking the header's printPrint Bill button opens a beautiful purple-headed panel labelled Generate Bill (or Print Bill, depending on the toggle). This panel does the heavy lifting of bill creation and reprinting.

The mode toggle

At the top right of the panel sits a glassy two-option toggle:

  • printPrint Bill — reprint bills that already exist (for a property, a street, or the whole system) for a chosen month.
  • receiptGenerate Bill — create new bills for a billing cycle. This is the action that actually runs a billing month.

The form fields and the helper text under the form change to match the mode you're in.

The four selectors

Below the header are four required pickers laid out two by two:

  • Street Selection — type to search. Selecting a street limits the property dropdown to properties on it.
  • Billing Month — defaults to the current month.
  • Property Subscription — pick a single property, or leave blank if you're billing more broadly.
  • Billing Year — defaults to the current year.

The two scope checkboxes

Below the selectors are two cards that control how wide the operation runs:

  • Process all properties on selected street — tick this and the Property dropdown is disabled. The action runs for every property on the chosen street.
  • Process all properties system-wide Coming soon — this option is visible in the form but is not active yet. For now, run the action either against a single property or street-by-street.

The action footer

At the bottom of the panel a friendly helper line tells you what is about to happen, like "Ready to generate bills for all properties on selected street". Beside it sits the action button, whose label and icon also depend on the mode:

  • receiptGenerate Bills when the toggle is on Generate.
  • printPrint Bills when the toggle is on Print.

What actually happens when you submit

  1. WastePro validates the form (street, month, year are required).
  2. For Generate Bill: the system creates new bills based on each property's billing details and updates outstanding balances. Duplicate-flag detection runs automatically. The dashboard's collection rate refreshes shortly after.
  3. For Print Bill: the system fetches the relevant existing bills, then takes you to a dedicated print-ready page (/print/{Month Year}) styled for an A4 printout. From there, press Ctrl+P (or ⌘+P on Mac) to send to a printer or save as PDF.
Automatic monthly billingBills are generated automatically by the system around the 25th or 26th of every month. You don't normally need to run Generate Bill yourself for the regular monthly cycle — use it for one-off corrections, re-runs after a property update, or to bring a newly added street current.

Payments

The Payments page is your collection log. The header has two action buttons on the right:

  • file_downloadExport CSV — download a daily payments file, useful for accounting or reconciliation.
  • add_cardPost New Payment — record a payment that came in by cash, transfer, or any other channel.

Filtering payments

Below the header is a Payment Filters card with four selectors:

  1. Month — pick the calendar month.
  2. Year — pick the year.
  3. Street — narrow to a specific street (start typing to search).
  4. Property — narrow further to a single property.

Each filter is optional. Combine any of them to focus the table below on exactly what you want to see.

Posting a payment

  1. Click add_cardPost New Payment.
  2. Pick the property and the bill the payment is for.
  3. Enter the amount, payment date, and any notes.
  4. Save. The payment shows up in the table and is reflected in the dashboard's collection rate.
Why this mattersEvery payment you post counts toward your collection rate metric. Keep this log clean and current.

Settings

Settings is where you configure how the system behaves. The page has up to two tabs depending on your role:

  • home_workProperty Types — the list of property types and their pricing. Visible to all staff with settings access.
  • securityAccess Control — the RBAC console for managing roles and staff. Visible only to super-admins.

Managing property types

Property types determine how much a property is billed (for example, Residential, Commercial, Industrial). The Property Types tab has:

  • A search box to find a type quickly. Press Ctrl+F to focus it, or Esc to clear it.
  • A addAdd button to define a new type.
  • A refreshRefresh button to reload the latest data.

To add a type, click addAdd, give it a name and a price, and save.

Access control · Super-admins only

The Access Control tab is your live RBAC console. It only shows up for super-admins to keep regular staff from changing their own roles. From here you can:

  • Toggle the entire RBAC system on or off with the Enable RBAC System switch.
  • Initialise the role and permission catalog the first time you turn RBAC on.
  • See the System Status card with role and permission counts.
  • View the User Management card listing every staff user, their roles, and edit/remove actions.
  • Click person_addAdd Staff User to invite a new teammate to your operator workspace. Service clients are intentionally excluded from this list because they don't have operator-app access.

For deeper role design (creating brand-new roles, editing permission sets), use Role Management (super-admin only).

User Access Management · Admins only

This page is your control room for who can do what. The left side shows Your Access — your roles and permissions in one card. The right side shows the Staff Users table — every operator-app user on your team.

Service clients aren't listed hereService clients (the property owners and residents who use the WastePro client portal) don't have operator-app access at all, so they're intentionally excluded from this page. Manage them from Properties & Billings instead.

Editing a user's roles

  1. Find the user in the Staff Users table (use search if needed).
  2. Click the editedit icon at the end of the row.
  3. Toggle roles on or off in the dialog that appears.
  4. Save. The change applies immediately the next time the user reloads.

Adding a new staff user

To invite a teammate to your operator workspace, head to Settings → Access Control and click person_addAdd Staff User on the User Management card. Fill in:

  • First Name and Last Name.
  • Email Address — this is what they'll sign in with.
  • Phone Number with country code.

The new user is created without any roles. Once they show up in the Staff Users table, click the editedit icon on their row to grant the roles they need.

Be carefulRemoving roles can lock a user out of pages they were using. Confirm with them first if it's not a routine change.

Role Management · Super-admins only

If User Access Management is about who has which role, Role Management is about what each role can do. The page splits into two cards side by side.

Creating a role

  1. On the left, give the role a name (e.g. Field Officer) and a short description.
  2. Tick the permissions the role should have. Each permission has a description so you know exactly what it grants.
  3. Click addCreate Role.

Editing or deleting roles

The right card lists existing roles. Each one has an editedit and a deletedelete button. Edit opens a dialog where you can change the name, description, or permissions. Delete removes the role entirely — do this only when no users are assigned to it.

Four cards across the top tell you the big picture:

  • Total Bills — how many bills have ever been issued to you.
  • Paid Bills — how many you have cleared.
  • Unpaid Bills — how many are still open.

Payments

Two things happen on this page: you see your dedicated virtual account, and you see your full payment history.

Your virtual account

The top card shows three things:

  • Account number — with a one-tap content_copycopy button.
  • Account name.
  • Bank name.

Send any payment to this account from your usual banking app. WastePro automatically detects it, applies it to your bill, and updates your records.

Why a virtual account?It saves you from typing reference codes or uploading proof of payment. Pay any amount, any time, from any bank, and your bill updates by itself.

Payment history

Below your virtual account you'll find a paginated list of every payment, with the amount, date, type (manual or virtual account), and status (paid, pending, failed). Filter by year using the dropdown.

Notifications

This is where you get bill reminders, payment confirmations, and operator announcements.

Three filter tabs sit at the top:

  • All — everything.
  • Unread — only the messages you haven't opened.
  • Alerts — important updates like overdue notices.

Each notification shows a title, a short description, the time it arrived, and (sometimes) an action button. You can:

  • Click the action button to jump to a relevant page.
  • Click Mark as read to clear the unread badge.
  • Click the deletedelete icon to remove a notification.
  • Or click done_allMark all as read at the top right to clear everything.

For New Operators · Onboarding

If you are a waste management company joining WastePro for the first time, you start at the public onboarding page. You don't need an account yet — you'll create one as part of this flow.

The two paths

  • New Waste Operator — if you've never registered before. Click businessRegister New Company and fill out your company details.
  • Existing Operator — if you started before but didn't finish. Type your registered email and click searchCheck Registration to pick up where you left off.

What happens next

  1. Register your company — name, contact, area of operation.
  2. Upload your data — lists of properties, streets, subscribers in CSV format.
  3. Review the summary — we show you what was imported and flag anything that needs fixing.
  4. Receive your credentials — once everything is verified, your operator admin account is activated and you can sign in like any other Waste Manager.
TipUse UTF-8 CSV files with a header row. Our team can help format your existing spreadsheet if you're not sure.

Common tasks

Quick recipes for the things you'll do most often.

Add a new street

  1. Open Settings (or the relevant area in Properties & Billings).
  2. Click the add button to open the Add Street dialog.
  3. Type the street name and pick its LGA.
  4. Save. The street is now available everywhere.

Add a new property

  1. Go to Properties & Billings → Properties tab.
  2. Click add_businessAdd Property.
  3. In the New Property Subscription dialog, fill in the property name, choose its street and type, then add subscriber details.
  4. Save.

Add a subscriber

  1. Open the Add Subscriber dialog (linked from the property details view, or directly from a New Property Subscription form).
  2. Fill in personal information — first name, last name, phone, email.
  3. Save. The subscriber gets a virtual account automatically.

Generate bills for a billing cycle

  1. Go to Properties & Billings → Billings tab.
  2. Click printPrint Bill.
  3. In the panel, switch the toggle to Generate Bill.
  4. Select street, billing period, and any other filters.
  5. Submit. Bills are created and a printable summary appears.

Adjust an outstanding balance

  1. Open the property's billing details.
  2. Find the bill and click the Edit Arrears action.
  3. Enter the new amount and a reason for the adjustment.
  4. Save. The change is logged.

Export daily payments

  1. Open Payments.
  2. Optionally filter by month, year, street, or property.
  3. Click file_downloadExport CSV.
  4. Pick the date range in the dialog and download.

FAQ & tips

I forgot my password. What now?

If you're a Service Client, you don't need a password — just sign in with your property code and phone number. If you're a Waste Manager, ask your operator's admin to reset your access from User Access Management.

Why don't I see all the menu items?

WastePro shows only the items your role allows. If you think you should see something extra (like Role Management or User Access), ask a super-admin to grant the right role.

Can I use WastePro on my phone?

Yes. The whole platform is mobile-friendly. The Service Client portal in particular is designed for phones first — the sidebar slides in from the left when you tap the menu icon.

Can I install WastePro like an app?

Yes. WastePro is a Progressive Web App. The first time you visit, your browser may show an install prompt. You can also use Chrome's "Install WastePro" option from the address bar.

How does virtual-account payment work?

Each Service Client gets a unique account number tied to their subscription. You can transfer to it from any bank or fintech app. WastePro receives the payment, matches it to your account, and updates your bill automatically — no need to send a screenshot or reference code.

The Collection Rate looks low. What can I do?

The two biggest levers are: (1) make sure every subscriber is on a virtual account so payments come in automatically; (2) use Notifications and reminders to nudge defaulters. The Payments page filters help you spot which streets and properties are lagging.

I clicked something and got an "Unauthorised" page.

That means your role doesn't have permission for that action. Either ask an admin to add the missing role, or sign in with an account that already has it.

How do I print a bill?

From Properties & Billings → Billings tab, click printPrint Bill, leave the toggle on Print Bill, choose the street and period, and submit. WastePro produces a print-ready page you can send to the printer with Ctrl+P.

Glossary

  • Operator — the waste management company running its business on WastePro.
  • Service Client / Subscriber — a property owner or resident receiving the service.
  • Property — an individual address being serviced.
  • Property Type — a billing category (Residential, Commercial, etc.) with a price attached.
  • LGA — Local Government Area; the largest geographic unit in WastePro's hierarchy.
  • Ward / Street — the geographic divisions inside an LGA used for billing and reporting.
  • Bill — an invoice issued to a subscriber for a billing period.
  • Arrears — outstanding amount on a bill.
  • Collection Rate — the percentage of billed amounts actually paid.
  • Virtual Account — a dedicated bank account number assigned to each subscriber for automated payments.
  • Role — a named set of permissions (e.g. Admin, Field Officer).
  • Permission — a specific action (e.g. billing:read) granted by a role.
